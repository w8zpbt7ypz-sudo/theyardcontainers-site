FAQ

Questions.

What does "wind & watertight" mean?

The container keeps weather out — no holes, working doors and seals. It may show dents and surface rust.

How much room do you need to deliver?

Roughly 70–80 ft of straight, level access for a 20' and 120–130 ft for a 40', plus overhead clearance.

Do I need a permit?

Rules vary by township and city. Check with your local zoning office before delivery.

Can I buy my rental later?

Yes — ask about rent-to-own.

What surface should it sit on?

Firm, level ground. Gravel or blocks under the corners work well.
