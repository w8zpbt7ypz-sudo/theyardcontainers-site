Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

The Yard Containers is a sole proprietorship owned and operated by Santiago Moncada, located at 5672 E Michigan Ave, Comstock (Kalamazoo), MI 49048 ("we," "us"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit theyardcontainers.com, place an order, call us, or receive text messages from us.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order and quote information: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, ZIP code, requested delivery date, order notes, and the container you select.
  • SMS consent: whether you opted in to receive text messages, and the date and phone number of that opt-in.
  • Phone calls: our AI phone receptionist takes orders by phone. Calls may be recorded and transcribed by our service providers to process your order and improve customer care.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number.
  • Basic technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting provider.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide quotes, process and fulfill orders, schedule and complete delivery, collect payment, provide customer care, and send account notifications such as order confirmations and delivery updates. We may also use it to comply with law and protect our rights.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed for that purpose: Render (website hosting), Google Workspace (email), Twilio (text messaging and phone service), Retell AI (AI voice receptionist), and Stripe (payments). We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

4. Text Messages

If you opt in, we send order and delivery alerts to your phone. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions for details.

5. Data Retention

We keep order, payment, and communication records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide customer care, and meet tax, accounting, and legal obligations, generally up to seven years, and then delete or de-identify them.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access-restricted accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, subject to records we must keep by law. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
